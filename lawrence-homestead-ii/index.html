Lawrence Homestead II

History

According to tour information, this house was bought in 1774 by Leonard Lawrence (1741-1821). The Lawrence family lived in it until 1874. In 1972 the house was moved to Old Bethpage Village on Long Island.

The color photos are of the restored house.
