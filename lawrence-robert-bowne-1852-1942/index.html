Lawrence, Robert Bowne (1852–1942)

1852–1942
Parents: John Watson Lawrence (1800–1888) and Mary King Bowne (1808–1874)
Spouse: Elizabeth Hyde Clements (1864–1949)
Children: Robert Rutherford Lawrence (1889–1952)
Kinship: Second cousin four times removed of the post-World War II Smith generation

Robert graduated from Columbia College and Columbia Law School, practiced law in Manhattan, and lived at Willow Bank in Flushing. He was active in Masonic affairs.
