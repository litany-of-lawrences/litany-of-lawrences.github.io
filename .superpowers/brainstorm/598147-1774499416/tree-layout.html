Family tree layout style

How should the tree be arranged on the page?

A — Top-down

Ancestors at the top, descendants flowing downward. Classic genealogy chart style. Works well for deep lineages but gets wide quickly.

B — Left-to-right

Ancestors on the left, descendants to the right. Familiar from org charts. Easier to read individual lineages; scrolls horizontally for large families.

C — Person-centred

Click any person to focus on them. Parents above, children below, siblings alongside. The tree redraws around whoever you select. Good for exploring rather than seeing everything at once.
