# Lawrence, Thomas (1588/89–1624/25)
Parents: —
Spouse: Joane Antrobus (1592–1661)
Children: [John Lawrence (1618–1699)](/lawrence-john-1618-1699/), Thomas Lawrence (1619–1703), William Lawrence (1622–1680), Jane Antrobus Lawrence (1614–1680), Mary Lawrence (1625–1715)
Kinship: Ninth great-grandfather of the post-World War II Smith generation

[Thomas Lawrence](/lawrence-thomas-1733-1817/) is the earliest identified ancestor of the post-World War II Smith generation. After his death, his widow Joane later remarried John Tuttell, who accompanied her and her sons to the Massachusetts Bay Colony aboard the Planter in 1635. This migration established the Lawrence family in America.
