# Lawrence, William (1823–1898)

**Parents:** William Effingham Lawrence (1782–1841) and of Mary Ann George Smither (1797–1880).

**Spouse:** Mary Isabella Lannon (1839–1875)

**Children:** Alfred William Lawrence (1862–1900), Cornelius Henry Lawrence (1864–1940), Maria Jane Lawence (1866–1926), Harriet Lawrence (1868–1943), Robert Effingham Lawrence (1869–1939), George Lawrence (1870–1943), Mary Isabella Lawrence (1872–1945), and Mary Jane Lawrence (1878–1926).

**Kinship:** Second cousin five times removed of the post-World War II Smith generation.

William Lawrence was born in Tasmania. He inherited the family estate [Formosa](/formosa/) when his brother died, but never lived there. He travelled extensively in Europe during 1851–1855.
