# Lawrence, William Effingham (1813–1871)

**Parents:** [Effingham Lawrence (1779–1850)](/lawrence-effingham-1779-1850/) and Anne Townsend (1782–1845).
**Spouse:** (1) Caroline Augusta Lawrence (1815–1841); (2) Rachel Augusta Mickle (1828–1863), daughter of Andrew Hutchins Mickle, Mayor of New York City (1846–47).
**Children:** Edward Newbold Lawrence (1841–1866); Caroline Augusta Lawrence (1849–1925) [needs full documentation]; Andrew Mickle Lawrence (1853–1888); Effie Lawrence (1856–1934) [needs full documentation]; William Effingham Lawrence (1857–1902); Grace Townsend Lawrence (1859–1929) [needs full documentation]; Louisa T. Lawrence (1863–1876).
**Kinship:** Third great-granduncle of the post–World War II Smith generation.

**Early Life and Family Background**
William [Effingham Lawrence](/lawrence-effingham-1760-1800/) was a member of the Bayside branch of the Lawrence family, descended from early Long Island settlers with longstanding commercial and marital ties to the Townsends. His first wife was his cousin, Caroline Augusta Lawrence, who died young in 1841. His second marriage to Rachel Augusta Mickle linked him to the New York City political world through her father, Andrew H. Mickle, a mid-nineteenth-century mayor.

**Residences and Family Connections**
He lived principally in New York and Bayside, Queens, where the Lawrence family maintained deep roots. The family is represented in the Lawrence Cemetery at Bayside, where William, his first wife, and several of his children are buried. The youngest child, Louisa T. Lawrence, is recorded on the family monument as having died in Detroit in 1876.

**Children and Generational Continuity**
His eldest son, [Edward Newbold Lawrence](/lawrence-edward-newbold-1805-1839/), died in 1866 at Funchal, Madeira, a common destination for convalescence.
