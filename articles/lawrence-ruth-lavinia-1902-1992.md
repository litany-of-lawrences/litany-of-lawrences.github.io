# Lawrence, Ruth Lavinia (1902–1992)

**Parents:** [William Miner Lawrence (1861–1935)](/lawrence-william-miner-1861-1935/) and Lavinia Olivia Oliver (1869–1916)
**Spouse:** M. Stuart Briggs (1901–1957)
**Children:** Stuart McCrate Briggs (1927–1989), [William Miner Lawrence](/lawrence-william-miner-1861-1935/) Briggs (1930– ), Rita Lavinia Briggs (1936–1989)
**Kinship:** Seventh cousin three times removed of the post-World War II Smith generation

Ruth graduated from Wellesley College in 1925 and married in 1926.
