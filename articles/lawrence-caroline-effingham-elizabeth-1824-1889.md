# Lawrence, Caroline Effingham Elizabeth (1824–1889)

**Parents:** Effingham Calvert Lawrence (1785–1837) and Caroline Munro (1795–?).

**Spouse:** None recorded.

**Children:** None recorded.
**Kinship:** Second cousin five times removed of the post–World War II Smith generation.

Caroline was born in the Maldives and died in Middlesex, England.

The  indicate that a Caroline Lawrence was admitted on 9 August 1852  to Grovesnor House, a private lunatic asylum. She was discharged 13 May 1882, “not improved.” If this is the same Caroline Lawrence, it would explain the lack of marriage.
