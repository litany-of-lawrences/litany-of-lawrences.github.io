# Lawrence, William Thomas (1817–1893)

**Parents:** [John Lawrence](/lawrence-john-1618-1699/) Lawrence (1785–1849) and Sarah Augusta Smith (1784–1877).
**Spouse:** Sophie Clinch Tilley (1832–1894).
**Children:** Sarah Lawrence (1854–1857); Sophia St. Germain Lawrence (1858– ); Alice Montgomery Lawrence (1863–1895); and Margaret Woodhull Lawrence (1866– ).
**Kinship:** Fifth cousin five times removed of the post–World War II Smith generation.

**Early Life and Family Background**
William [Thomas Lawrence](/lawrence-thomas-1733-1817/) was born in New York City on February 7, 1817. He was a member of the long-established Lawrence and Smith families of New York. Through his mother, he was descended from the Smiths of Smithtown, Long Island.

**Career**
Lawrence spent his career in New York financial institutions. He served as cashier of the Institution for the Savings of Merchants’ Clerks, a prominent mutual savings bank founded in 1820 to encourage thrift among mercantile employees. The annual reports of the Institution describe the cashier as a senior administrative and fiduciary officer, responsible for day-to-day financial operations, bookkeeping oversight, and custodianship of funds.

**Later Life and Death**
He lived most of his life in Manhattan, later residing in Brooklyn. William [Thomas Lawrence](/lawrence-thomas-1733-1817/) died in Brooklyn on November 5, 1893, at the age of 76.
