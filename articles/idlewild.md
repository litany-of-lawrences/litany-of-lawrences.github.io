# Idlewild

![idlewild](/images/idlewild-1.png)

**Location:** In the vicinity of present-day Idlewild Drive in Morris Plains, New Jersey.

**Built:** c.1890.

**History**

It was built in the 1890s and became the temporary home of Louis Andrew Thebaud while his grander house, [Beauregard](/beauregard/), was being built. In the 1920s Idlewild was purchased by Lewis Grove and became his development’s country club. It burned to the ground in 1925 after an American Legion party.
