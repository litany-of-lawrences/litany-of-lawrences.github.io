# Alexandre, Cynthia Lenox Banks (1944– )

**Parents:** [Dewitt Loomis Alexandre (1917–1982)](/alexandre-dewitt-loomis-1917-1982/) and Cynthia [Lenox](/lenox/) Banks (1918–1991). Spouse: Edmund Pendleton Rogers III (1941– ).

**Children:** Edmund Pendleton Rogers IV (1972– ).

**Kinship:** Second cousin of the post–World War II Smith generation.

**Early Life and Family** Cynthia [Lenox](/lenox/) Banks Alexandre was born in 1944, the daughter of [Dewitt Loomis Alexandre](/alexandre-dewitt-loomis-1917-1982/) and Cynthia [Lenox](/lenox/) Banks. She belongs to the branch of the Alexandre family descended from the prominent New York shipping merchant [Frederick Francis Alexandre](/alexandre-frederick-francis-1809-1889/).

**Career and Activities** A graduate of Smith College, she spent part of her professional life in London, where she was associated with the Alexandre family’s continuing international connections. During that period she attended the funeral of her distant relative, Sir Winston Churchill, as a representative of the Alexandre family.

**Residences** In later life, Cynthia Alexandre has made her home in Gulf Stream, Florida.
