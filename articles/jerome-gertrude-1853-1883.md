# Jerome, Gertrude (1853–1883)

**Parents:** Thomas Atwater Jerome (1810–1896) and Emma Vanderbilt (1818–1901).
**Married:** [James Henry Alexandre (1848–1912)](/alexandre-james-henry-1848-1912/).
**Children:** Gertrude Jerome Alexandre (1879–1949) and [James Henry Alexandre](/alexandre-james-henry-1848-1912/) Jr. (1883–1956).
**Kinship:** Great-grandmother of the post–World War II Smith generation.

**Biography**
Gertrude Jerome was born in Brooklyn in 1853, the daughter of Thomas Atwater Jerome and Emma Vanderbilt, members of a well-connected New York family. She married [James Henry Alexandre](/alexandre-james-henry-1848-1912/) in the late 1870s and lived principally in New York City and at Shore Acres on Staten Island. Her short married life was marked by the births of two children: a daughter in 1879 and a son in 1883.

Her death occurred on 30 April 1883 in Manhattan, the day after the birth of her son. She was twenty-nine years old.
