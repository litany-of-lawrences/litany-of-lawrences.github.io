# Lawrence, William Effingham (1888–1974)

![lawrence-william-effingham-1888-1974](/images/lawrence-william-effingham-1888-1974-1.jpeg)

**Parents**: William Effingham Lawrence (1857–1902) and Emma Bradford Rose (1863–1920).

**Spouse**: Anita Livingston Ord (1894–1955).

**Children**:  William [Effingham Lawrence](/lawrence-effingham-1760-1800/) III (1922–2011), Jean Ord Lawrence (1926–) and Roger Ord Lawrence (1928–2001).

**Kinship**: Second cousin  three times removed of the post-World War II Smith generation.

He attended school in Toms River, New Jersey, and worked as a realtor. He was a member of St Peter’s Episcopal Church in Port Chester.
